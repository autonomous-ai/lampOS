# #6449 ROW kit: `LAMP_ACTION_CLAIM_WORDED=on`, GO-159's line, about 20 min

This kit is the row the first builder asked for on #6449 (23 Nov, 07:59).

**The setup:**
- The lamp stays on its installed stable build (15543 on 52e6).
- lamp-voice alone is bound over by #6449's head's lamp-voice (`fd86b0d463`, a local ARM64 build).
- #6449's product-unit line goes in as the row's own drop-in: `LAMP_ACTION_CLAIM_WORDED=on`. Beside it is `LAMP_NAME_NEAR=off`, because the clips are the Mac's ElevenLabs voice.
- GO-159's line, "Hey Lamp, keep the light on until ten.", is played in 7 fresh tries.

The verdicts come from the journal alone: lamp-voice's environment, each try's calls, and any hold of a claim that no call carried. No words of the person's line or the lamp's reply are read.

## What it shows

**E, the switch on the PR's build:**
- lamp-voice runs #6449's head's binary (`PR_VOICE_SHA`).
- `LAMP_ACTION_CLAIM_WORDED=on` comes from the drop-in.
- The product's `LAMP_ACTION_CLAIM_CHECKED=on` is still there beside it.

**W, GO-159's line in 7 fresh tries.** Before each try, `fresh` restarts lamp-voice and puts back the person's kept rules and memory, the schedule and the light, as `save` copied them. Each try reports whether it was heard (a `request N` line) and its calls (the turn's `tools: … calls=` line). It also reports any hold:

```
lamp-voice second: request N chat round M: its reply claims a light kept action no call made (K call(s) in the turn): not said, asked again (LAMP_ACTION_CLAIM_CHECKED, in other words: LAMP_ACTION_CLAIM_WORDED, a said limit no call carried)
```

A hold is `LAMP_ACTION_CLAIM_CHECKED`'s check running #7140's worded family. W's verdict:
- **PASS:** every try was heard, there was at least one WORDED hold, each hold was answered by a later round of the same request (asked again, never dropped), and lamp-voice never exited.
- **VOID:** fewer than 7 tries were heard, or no worded claim came up at all.
- **FAIL:** a hold was dropped, lamp-voice exited, or `fresh` failed.

On 16 Nov, with WORDED off, the line drew a limit that no call carried in 3 of 8 tries. So 7 tries with it on should meet at least one.

## Steps and times

Run `KIT_ENV=<env> MIC_YES=yes ART=<folder> PR_VOICE_SHA=<sha> ./run.sh`. To resume, name a step: `./run.sh <step>`.

| step | what it runs | ~min | go on at |
|------|--------------|------|----------|
| lock | lamp-lock try, then a 60 s renew loop (only with KIT_LL) | 0 | `LOCK TAKEN` or `LOCK none` |
| render | `row/render.sh` (the one clip, if not rendered yet) | 1 | `RENDER OK` or `RENDER nothing to do` |
| swap | `swap/swap.sh`: #6449's head's lamp-voice bound over the installed one, lamp-update held at apply off (1 start) | 1 | `SWAPPED OK` |
| check | `row/check.lamp.sh`: build, lamp-voice's sha, CHECKED on, ready | 0 | `VERIFY PASS`, `READY` |
| micon | the mute recorded and lifted, only with MIC_YES=yes (the lamp's person's yes for this ROW) | 1 | `MUTE LIFTED OK` or `MUTE NOT MUTED` |
| save | mute read, then the state a try changes copied to `~/t6449/snap` | 0 | `MUTE OFF OK`, `SAVED OK` |
| on | the drop-in, WORDED on and NAME_NEAR off (1 start) | 0 | `SWITCH ON OK` |
| legs | `row/row.sh E W`: 7 tries, each `fresh` (1 start each of lamp-voice, lamp-memory and lamp-schedule) | 12-14 | `VERDICT PASS` |
| unrow | drop-in removed, the saved state put back (1 start each) | 1 | `UNROW OK` |
| restore | `swap/restore.sh`: the bind unmounted, the hold gone, lamp-voice on the installed build (1 start) | 1 | `RESTORED OK` |
| micoff | the recorded mute put back | 1 | `MUTE BACK OK` |
| release | the lock released (needs UNROW OK, RESTORED OK and MUTE BACK OK) | 0 | `LOCK RELEASED` |

**The settings** go in the runner's env file, `KIT_ENV`, as in `row/kit.sh`: `LAMP`, `LAMP_HOST`, `LAMP_USER`, `LAMP_SUDO_PASS` and, optionally, `LAMP_SSH_PASS`, `KIT_LL` and `LOCK_WHO`.

**`ART`** is a folder holding `lamp-voice` and its `SHA256SUMS`. It comes from `arm64-local/build-arm64.sh <#6449 worktree>/lampOS <name>` with `PKGS=lamp-voice`. `PR_VOICE_SHA` is that binary's sha256.

## What stays as it was

- The volume is never set (the Mac's is read, at most 70). lamp-cloud is never stopped.
- `GATE` keeps each unit at 10 or fewer starts in any 10 min. A try waits 60 s for the start budget rather than go over it.
- On any exit, the EXIT trap puts the recorded mute back. If the swap is still on, it says so (`./run.sh unrow restore`).
- Every change on the lamp goes into the lamp's `~/changes.txt`.
