lamp-eyes --device ABSOLUTE_PATH --lock-directory PRIVATE_ABSOLUTE_DIRECTORY --usb VENDOR:PRODUCT@TOPOLOGY --model ABSOLUTE_PATH --permit-socket ABSOLUTE_PATH --body-socket ABSOLUTE_PATH [--sight-file ABSOLUTE_PATH] [--presence-file ABSOLUTE_PATH] [--recogniser ABSOLUTE_PATH --people ABSOLUTE_PATH --who-socket ABSOLUTE_PATH [--known-file ABSOLUTE_PATH]] [--leave-still-faces on|off] [--steady-rate on|off] [--look-on-change on|off] [--full-look WIDTHxHEIGHT --full-look-file ABSOLUTE_PATH [--full-look-movement on|off]] [--mouth-model ABSOLUTE_PATH [--mouth-on-heard on|off] [--mouth-runtime ABSOLUTE_PATH]]

Owns the camera at --device (checked against the USB identity given, hex vendor and
product and the port topology, for example 01da:5875@3-1.1.4) and looks for a face in
its frames four times a second while there is one and less than twice a second when
there is none, with the pinned YuNet model at --model. With --steady-rate on, it looks
twice a second while one face has sat still for 3 s (off unless given). With
--look-on-change on, once no face has been found for 4 s, a frame like the last one the
detector ran on is said to hold no face without it, and the detector runs at least every
3 s (off unless given). Each look
sends one datagram to --body-socket: {"face":{"x":..,"y":..,"size":..,"turned":..}} or
{"face":null}. The camera is open only while {"camera":true} keeps arriving on
--permit-socket, at least every 1.5 s; {"camera":false} closes it at once. Without
--sight-file and --full-look-file no frame is written anywhere and none leaves this process. With it the
newest frame, and only that one, is kept in that file (mode 0600) while the camera is
open and removed when it closes: it is what the voice service shows the model. With
--presence-file one line, the near face's height as a share of the picture's, is kept
fresh in that file while there is a face: no picture, only that number. With
--recogniser (the pinned SFace model), --people and --who-socket, the eyes remember
a face when the voice service asks ({"remember":"NAME"} on the socket), forget it
({"forget":"NAME"}), list the names ({"list":true}) and say whose face is at the
lamp ({"who":true}). Only the prints of people who asked are kept, in --people
(mode 0600); no picture. With --known-file, the name of the one known at the lamp is
kept fresh in that file while they are there. With --leave-still-faces on, a face at a place
where faces have come from for half an hour, every one small (under 0.14 of the height)
and faint (scored under 0.8), is not a person: it is left out of what the body is told,
the bus and recognition (off unless given). With --full-look and --full-look-file the
camera runs at that size, the working frame is kept in --sight-file at 320 across as before, and {"look_full":ID} on
--permit-socket asks for one still: taken once the head has settled
(body/joint_states on the bus, at most 2 s), at most 1024 across and 64 KiB, or with
{"look_full":ID,"side":PIXELS} at most that side across and 184 KiB, marked
with the ID in a JPEG comment, kept in --full-look-file for 15 s and removed (off unless given).
With --full-look-movement on the head settles on movement only: the camera's aim, the arm's
joints with the head's, and never a joint's distance to its goal (off unless given).
With --mouth-model (the pinned Face Mesh V2), the journal says how open the largest
face's mouth is each look, and with LAMP_EYES_SPEAKING=on that face's speaking reads it
(off unless given). With --mouth-on-heard on, the mesh runs only while the voice hears a
line (voice/heard on the bus, from its first words until 2 s after it ends).
With --mouth-runtime (the voice's pinned ONNX Runtime library, checked before it is loaded),
the mesh runs under ONNX Runtime on one thread instead of tract.
