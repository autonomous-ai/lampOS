1

Plug me in, then open the app

About five minutes. Have your Wi-Fi password ready.

  1. Put me where you usually sit

    A desk or a bedside table works best, a little to one side of you. Then plug me in.

  2. Open the Autonomous app and tap Add Lamp

    Your phone talks to me directly for a moment, over my own little network called lamp- and a few letters.

  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi

    Type its password. If I can't join, the app tells you, and you can try again.

  4. Wait for my hello

    I take a few minutes to get my voice ready. Then I'll say hello, and we start.

  • A soft glow: I'm waking up.
  • Blinking blue: I'm joining your Wi-Fi.

2

Our first five minutes

I lead, one short step at a time. Say "skip" for any step, and I'll offer it again later.

  1. The privacy switch

    I show you where it is. Slide it and both my microphones are off, until you slide it back.

  2. Sit where you usually sit

    Look at me for a moment, so I learn where you'll be.

  3. Tell me your name

    I say it back, so you can check I heard it right. If I didn't, spell it for me.

  4. Your face, if you like

    Say yes and I'll remember your face, so I know it's you next time. Say no and I'll still remember your name.

  5. Call me a few times

    Say Hey Lamp from your seat, the way you would on any day.

  6. A tap on my head

    OK, next step. Tap the top of my head once, then let go.

  7. A gentle tilt

    Hold my base with one hand. With the other, tilt my head gently to one side. Let go when I say so.

  8. Quiet hours, and hellos

    Tell me when to keep quiet, and whether I may say hello when you come back to your desk.

Changed your mind later? Say Lamp, let's redo the setup.

3

Talk to me like a friend at your desk

Start with "Hey Lamp", then ask in your own words.

  • Set a timer for ten minutes.I ring when it's up, even if we're mid-chat.
  • Remind me to call Mom at six.Ask "What reminders do I have?" any time.
  • Make the light warmer.Brighter, dimmer, or a colour you like.
  • Why is the sky blue?Ask me anything you'd ask a friend.

Talking too long? Say Lamp, stop.

4

Give me a new skill

In the app: your lamp, then Skills, then Store. Tap Add, and I have it.

Every skill in the Store is checked and signed before it gets there. Try Look for things first:

Lamp, where are my keys?

I look around your desk, a few places either side, and tell you what I find.

Right after setup, I quietly download my first update. If you don't see Add yet, give me about 15 minutes, then look again.

6

Build with code

For developers: Python or JavaScript. Start with a pretend lamp on your own computer.

# get the SDK
$ git clone https://github.com/autonomous-ai/lampOS
$ python3 -m venv ~/lampos-venv && . ~/lampos-venv/bin/activate
$ pip install './lampOS/sdk/python[check]'

# a pretend lamp, no hardware needed
$ LAMPOS_HOME=/tmp/lampos-try lamp fake

# later, your real lamp
$ lamp pair CODE --pin PIN
$ lamp hello
  1. Add your computer in the app

    Developer computers, then Add. Choose what your programs may do. The app shows a code and my pin.

  2. Pair from your computer

    Run lamp pair with that code and pin. I give a small nod when we're paired.

  3. Remove it any time

    Revoke your computer in the app, and its connection closes at once.

Developer computers arrives in the app with its next update; the pretend lamp works today. Full guide: Program your lamp in 10 minutes.
