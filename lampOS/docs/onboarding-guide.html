Getting started

Welcome to your Lamp

A lamp you can talk to. It learns who you are, helps with your day, and you can teach it new things. Here is how to go from the box to your first skill.

Step 1 · about 10 minutes

Set up your lamp

  1. Plug it in

    Put the lamp where you usually sit, then plug it in. A soft light shows it is waking up.

  2. Open the Autonomous app and tap Add Lamp

    Your phone connects to the lamp for a moment. Its network is called lamp- and a few letters.

  3. Choose your home Wi-Fi

    Pick your network and type its password. The lamp blinks blue while it joins. If it can't join, the app tells you, and you can try again.

  4. Give it a few minutes

    The lamp gets its voice ready. Then it says hello, and the two of you start your first five minutes together.

Step 2

Talk to it

Say Hey Lamp, then ask in your own words. Some first things to try:

  • Set a timer for ten minutes.It rings when time's up.
  • Remind me to call Mom at six.Ask "What reminders do I have?" any time.
  • Make the light warmer.Or brighter, dimmer, a colour you like.
  • Why is the sky blue?Ask anything you'd ask a friend.
To stop it mid-sentence, say Lamp, stop.

Step 3

Add a skill from the Store

  1. Open Skills in the app

    Choose your lamp, then Skills, then Store.

  2. Tap Add

    The lamp installs the skill in a moment. Every skill in the Store is checked and signed before it gets there.

  3. Use it

    Try Look for things: say Lamp, where are my keys? and it looks around your desk for them.

In the Store today:

  • Look for things
  • Comfort break
  • Meal check
  • Sit back
Right after setup, your lamp quietly downloads its first update. If you don't see Add yet, give it about 15 minutes, then look again.

Step 4

Make your own skill

Tell the lamp what you want in one sentence. It writes the skill itself, then asks if it should turn it on.

  • By voiceLamp, make me a skill that dims the light at 10 pm.
  • In the appSkills, then Make a skill. Type what you want, the same way you'd say it.

Skills you make can use the lamp's light and its movement. Keep the idea short and specific.

Step 5

Share it with everyone

  1. Open the skill you made

    In the app: Skills, then the skill.

  2. Tap Share

    It goes to the Store team. We review every shared skill before it appears in the Store for others.

Step 6 · for developers

Build with the SDK

Program your lamp from your computer, in Python or JavaScript. Start with a pretend lamp on your own machine:

git clone https://github.com/autonomous-ai/lampOS
python3 -m venv ~/lampos-venv && . ~/lampos-venv/bin/activate
pip install './lampOS/sdk/python[check]'
LAMPOS_HOME=/tmp/lampos-try lamp fake

Then connect your real lamp:

  1. Add your computer in the app

    Developer computers, then Add. Choose what your programs may do. The app shows a code and your lamp's pin.

  2. Pair from your computer

    lamp pair CODE --pin PIN, then lamp hello. The lamp gives a small nod when your computer pairs.

  3. Remove it any time

    Revoke your computer in the app and its connection closes at once.

If Developer computers isn't in your app yet, it arrives with the next app update. The pretend lamp works today.
