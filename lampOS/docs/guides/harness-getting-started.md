# Get your lamp working with your computer: Harness

Your lamp can hand work to the AI helpers on your computer, such as Claude Code. You ask out loud, and the lamp tells you when the work is done. A program called **Harness** carries the work from your lamp to your computer. Setting it up takes about ten minutes.

## What you can ask your lamp to do

You talk to the lamp as you always do. The lamp says back what it understood, and nothing starts until you say yes.

1. **Ask for a piece of work.** "Ask Claude Code to write a poem about the rain." The lamp repeats the task. You say yes. The lamp tells you Claude Code has it, and that it will tell you when it is done.
2. **Ask for something real from your work.** "Ask Claude Code to look at why my build is slow and tell me what it finds." The lamp gives the work to your computer.
3. **Hear how it went, without asking.** When the helper finishes, the lamp tells you at a good moment, in its own words, with what the helper found. If the helper stops to ask you something, the lamp tells you that too, and you answer at your computer.

Good to know:

- The lamp only gives work to the helper you name. If you name one that isn't set up on your computer, the lamp says so and gives the work to nobody else.
- Nothing starts without your spoken yes to the exact task.
- The lamp tells you what the helper reported. It can't check that the work is right.
- Work goes to a **computer**. The Harness phone app lets you watch and approve, but it doesn't do the work. Your lamp hands tasks to agents on a computer signed in to Harness.

## Set it up in three steps

You need the Autonomous app on your phone, a computer you want the lamp to use, and a lamp that is already set up and online. You don't install anything on the lamp: it gets Harness by itself once it is online.

### Step 1. Install Harness on your computer

Download Harness for your computer at [harness.autonomous.ai/desktop](https://harness.autonomous.ai/desktop). It runs on macOS 12 or later, Windows 10 or 11, and Ubuntu 22.04 or later. On Windows, the installer is not signed yet, so choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### Step 2. Sign in to Harness

Open Harness on your computer and sign in. You can continue with Google, Apple or email, or scan the code with your phone.

### Step 3. Connect Harness to your lamp

1. In the Autonomous app, open your lamp's Harness card and start the connection. The app shows an 8-digit code.
2. In Harness on your computer, open **Machines**, then **Approve a device**, and type the code. Press **Continue**.
3. Harness asks "Sign in" with your lamp's name. Press **Approve**. If your computer isn't on the same network as the lamp, hold the button for 2 seconds instead, after reading the warning.
4. The Autonomous app asks "Sign in as" and shows your email. Choose **Yes** if it is yours.

You can also type the code at [harness.autonomous.ai](https://harness.autonomous.ai) if you are signed in there, or in the Harness phone app under **Settings**, then **Sign in a computer**, then **Type a code instead**.

That's it. In a minute or two the lamp knows your computer, and you can ask it for work.

- If you take too long, the code runs out. Start again from the Autonomous app.
- If you choose **No**, or the app says it didn't work, nothing is connected. Start again from the app.
- Someone who overhears the code gets nothing from it. The lamp connects only after **you** answer yes to your own email.
- You can close the Harness window. Harness keeps running in the background. After your computer restarts, open Harness once (Windows starts it for you).

## Try it

Say: "Ask Claude Code to write a short poem about the rain." Say yes when the lamp asks. In a little while, the lamp tells you how it went.

## If something doesn't work

- **The lamp says it isn't set up to hand work over yet.** Harness isn't connected. Do step 3 again.
- **The lamp says it can't find a computer.** The lamp learns your computers from Harness. Check that Harness is running and signed in on the computer, and wait a minute.
- **You named a helper the lamp says isn't set up.** Set that helper up on your computer, or ask for one that is.
