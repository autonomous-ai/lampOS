# Guard mode

While you're out, the lamp tells you by text when someone is at your desk:
"Someone was at your desk at 14:31, for about 2 minutes." At most one text
every ten minutes.

**On and off.** You turn it on by asking out loud, at the lamp: "Lamp, keep
an eye on things while I'm out." The lamp says in one short line that it's
on, and the ring keeps a faint blue mark while it is, so nobody is watched
without a sign. If another program's light hides the mark, the lamp asks
for it again, and counts nothing until it shows. If you turn your own
light off, it pauses, texts you that it has, and counts nothing: it never
puts its mark over a light you chose. It goes on when you ask for it again
in words, not when your light simply comes back on. It starts counting once
you've left the desk. It goes off when you say stop; when the lamp sees
your own face again, if it knows your face, and then it says "I'm off
guard"; or by itself after twelve hours, with a text: "I'm off guard". It
never turns itself on, and a typed message doesn't turn it on: it needs to
hear you ask for it. Where the lamp allows it, you can also turn it on and
off from the app; the mark shows all the same.

**What it says.** Only when someone came, about when and for how long. Who
it was is said only as "someone you've introduced me to" or "someone I
don't know": in a shared home, a name would tell you whom another person
visited. Where the lamp allows names, someone whose face it knows and who
said yes to being named is named: "Minh and someone the lamp knows came in
at 18:10". Nobody is ever named from their voice, and someone who takes
back their yes isn't named again, not even in a text still to go.

**Someone coming in.** Where the lamp allows it, a door (opened, shut or
knocked on) or footsteps count as someone coming in, but only when a face
or a voice follows within half a minute: a door alone texts nothing. The
text then says they came in: "Someone I don't know came in at 14:31 and was
here for about 2 minutes."

**What it uses.** Whether anyone is at the lamp (counts and times only), the
size and place of the faces in view, whether a face is one the lamp knows,
and, where allowed, how sure the ears are that they heard a door, footsteps
or speech. It records nothing: it reads no picture or sound, keeps none,
and sends none. As someone comes in the lamp turns its head to them once,
as a creature would, then rests, and not again within two minutes. It
doesn't patrol or scan the room, and doesn't flash; it says nothing but
"I'm off guard". It answers anyone who speaks to it as usual.

**The privacy switch ends it.** With the switch closed the lamp can't see:
it stops keeping an eye on things at once and texts you once, "Guard is
off: the privacy switch is closed." Ask again once it's open.

If asked about it: "While you're out I keep an eye on your desk and text
you if someone comes by. I don't record anything, and I never say who. Say
stop and I'll stop."
