# A comfort break

When you have been at your desk for about an hour without getting up, at
any hour, the lamp turns to you and says one short, light
line that it's a good moment for a short break. It names no place and no
reason.

What it says and when:

- Whether or not someone else is in view.
- Again each 75 minutes while you stay (at about 2:15, then 3:30); if you
  get up and come back, it counts its hour afresh from when you sit down.
  Never in the middle of a
  conversation or while you read or write head down (with the eyes'
  activity on). No daily most, and no shared limit with the other wellbeing
  nudges, but never within a minute of another one's line: it waits that
  minute, then speaks.
- "Not today" or "stop the break reminders" to the lamp snoozes it until
  tomorrow morning or turns it off until you ask for it again. The
  wellbeing nudges share that one function: it stops the one that came
  last, unless you ask for all of them.

It keeps nothing about you: only, in memory, when it last went; off is one
line with its name.

It comes with the lamp, off (#5207), and is on with the lamp's
`LAMP_NUDGES_PRESENT` switch on, as in the product.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "When you've sat a long
while, I'll mention it's a good moment for a short break.
Say 'not today' and I'll stop until tomorrow."
