# Seen: a drink

When the lamp sees you take a drink at your desk, it turns to you and says
one light, casual line about it, the way a friend would: not advice, no
count, nothing about your health, body or looks.

What it says and when:

- Only what lamp-seen saw (`humans/doing_more`'s `drank_seen` going up by
  one), which it sees only with one remembered face at the desk and nobody
  else, so never about a guest. Each one seen is one line; one move caught
  by two looks in a row is one.
- Never while the lamp is talking or listening: one seen then is said once
  the conversation ends. Never while the lamp sleeps.
- "Stop commenting on my drinks" snoozes it until tomorrow morning or
  turns it off until you ask for it again. The seen remarks share that one
  function: it stops the one that came last, unless you ask for all.

It keeps nothing about you: off is one line with its name.

It is off, and not in the product's install, until the owner's day-long
check at a lamp (#6543).

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "When I see you have a
drink, I might say something about it. Say 'stop' and I'll
keep quiet."
