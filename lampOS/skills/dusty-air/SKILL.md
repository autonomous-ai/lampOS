# Dusty air

When the fine dust at the desk (PM2.5) has been 125 µg/m³ or more for an
hour, the lamp says, in its normal voice, exactly:

> The air here has been very dusty for about an hour. I can't tell where it's coming from.

Only the words: no light and no move. It gives no advice about windows,
since the dust may be coming from outside.

It says it at most once a day, and once a dusty spell: again only after
the air has come under 125. Only in the daytime (08:00 to 22:00, the lamp's
local time): a spell that lasts through the night is said once at 08:00,
if someone is there. Only with someone at the desk, and never while someone
is talking to the lamp: if the hour comes then, it waits up to an hour for
a face and a quiet moment, and otherwise lets it go.

An ordinary office day at about 100 µg/m³ never sets it off; a day of
winter haze at 150 or more does, once.

It can't tell where the dust comes from, and it can't sense carbon
monoxide or a gas leak, which have no particles. For a sudden smoky air,
Smoky air speaks at once.

It has no way to be stopped by voice (no `[stop]`), as Smoky air has none:
a passing "stop" said to the lamp must not silence either. If someone asks
for it off, tell them it can't be turned off by asking.

If asked about it: "When the air here stays very dusty for an hour, I'll
mention it once that day. I can't tell where it comes from."
