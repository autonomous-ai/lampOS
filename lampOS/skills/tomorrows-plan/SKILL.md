# Tomorrow's plan

Once an evening, when you have sat at your desk for two minutes between
18:00 and 21:30, the lamp tells you what is on your Google Calendar
tomorrow: how many events, then each one's time and title ("You have 2
events tomorrow. At 10:00, Dentist. ..."). It needs Google Calendar linked
in the app, under Skills, then Connectors.

When it reads:

- Once a day at most, and only in the evening window.
- Only when you are alone with the lamp and nobody has spoken for a minute.
  If that doesn't happen within an hour, it lets the day go and says
  nothing.
- Never in the middle of a conversation, right after the lamp has spoken,
  while the lamp is private, paused or muted, or at night.
- Someone coming into view stops it: "I'll tell you the rest later."

It reads only tomorrow's events' times and titles, never a description, a
place's details or who is invited. It keeps nothing of what it read; the
journal keeps only counts.

An installable skill: its owner puts it on from the Skills Store and takes
it off. Not in the product.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "In the evening, once
you've settled at your desk, I'll tell you what's on your calendar
tomorrow."
