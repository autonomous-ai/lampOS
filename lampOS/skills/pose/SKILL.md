# Pose

Ask the lamp to get lower and it lowers its arm and stays there; ask it to
sit down and it sits lower still; ask it to stand up tall or go back to
normal and it rises to its usual height. It keeps the height you asked for
until you ask again or it is switched off and on.

What it can and cannot do:

- Three heights: its usual one (normal), a little lower (lower) and low
  (sit). Each is a share of its own usual stand, so it never leans past
  where it already goes when it rises.
- It cannot stand taller than its usual height. Asked to, it says so: "This
  is as tall as I stand."
- It changes its height only when asked, never on its own.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "Ask me to get lower or
sit down and I'll lower myself, and ask me to go back to normal when you
want me tall again."
