# Rest your eyes

When you have been at your desk for three quarters of an hour, between
09:00 and 20:00, the lamp gives a small curious tilt, as if something across
the room caught its eye, and its light softens a shade for the three
seconds of the tilt: a cue to look up from the screen and rest your eyes on
something far away. Then the light is as it was.

The lamp sees faces, not screens: "screen time" here is time sitting at
the desk.

What it says and when:

- It says nothing, ever.
- Once an hour at most while you stay, four times a day at most, and never
  in the middle of a conversation, before 09:00, after 20:00 or at night.
- At most one of the wellbeing nudges (this, A sip of water, Stand up and
  Wind down) an hour between them.
- If it does it twice and you neither look at it nor move nor say
  anything, it rests until tomorrow morning.
- "Not today" or "stop the eye breaks" to the lamp snoozes it until
  tomorrow morning or turns it off until you ask for it again.

It keeps nothing about you: only, in memory, how many times it went today
and whether it is resting; off is one line with its name.

An installable skill: its owner puts it on the lamp (`lamp-skills install`)
and takes it off. Not in the product yet.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "Every so often, when
you've been at the screen a while, I soften my light a moment, a hint to
look up and rest your eyes. Tell me 'not today' and I'll leave it."
