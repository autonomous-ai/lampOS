# Seen: eating

When the lamp sees you eating at your desk, it turns to you and says one
friendly, casual sentence about it, at most twenty words, the way a friend
would: enjoy it, or a light question about the meal. Not advice, no count,
nothing about your health, diet, body or looks.

What it says and when:

- Only what lamp-seen saw (`humans/doing_more`'s `ate_seen` going up by
  one), which it sees only with one remembered face at the desk and nobody
  else, so never about a guest. One move caught by two looks in a row is
  one.
- Once a meal: a meal ends after 30 minutes with no eating
  seen, and the next one seen after that is a new one. Another kind may
  come sooner, and a line you never heard doesn't count.
- Never while the lamp is talking or listening: one seen then is said once
  the conversation ends, or let go 3 minutes after it was seen. Never while
  the lamp sleeps, and never with the privacy slide closed: closing it
  lets go of anything seen before. Night or morning is no different.
- "Don't mention my eating" snoozes it until tomorrow morning or
  turns it off until you ask for it again. The seen remarks share that one
  function: it stops the one that came last, unless you ask for all.

It keeps nothing about you: off is one line with its name.

It comes with every lamp, on, by the owner's call (#6543), and says
nothing until the owner says yes to the lamp noticing more at the desk.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "When I see you eating,
I might say something about it. Say 'stop' and I'll keep
quiet."
