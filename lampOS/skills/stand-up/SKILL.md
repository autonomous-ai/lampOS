# Stand up

When you have been at your desk for seventy minutes without getting up,
at any hour, the lamp turns to you and gives a
small curious tilt, and says one short line: shall we? It is later than Stretch break's stretch (at
fifty minutes) and reads your sitting the same way, from your face at the
desk; a short look away does not end it, getting up for a few minutes does.

What it says and when:

- It says one short, friendly line, worded by the voice the way a friend
  would, and never anything about how you sit.
- Once a sitting, and not again within 75 minutes, with no daily most;
  never while you read or write head down (with the eyes' activity on), and
  never in the middle of a conversation.
- With the eyes' activity on, the sitting is yours: someone else sitting down in
  your chair begins it again, so it is never theirs at your 70 minutes.
- No shared limit with the other wellbeing nudges, but never within a
  minute of another one's line: it waits that minute, then speaks.
- "Not today" or "stop the stand-up reminders" to the lamp (one function
  for the wellbeing nudges: the one that came last, or all of them) snoozes it until
  tomorrow morning or turns it off until you ask for it again.

It keeps nothing about you: only, in memory, when it last went; off is one line with its name.

A core skill: it comes with the lamp, on, and can be turned off by voice
or in the app.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "When you've sat a really
long time I give you a little look and suggest you get up and move. Say 'not
today' and I'll stop until tomorrow."
