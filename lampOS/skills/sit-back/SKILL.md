# Sit back

When you have leaned in toward your desk for about a minute, the lamp
turns to you and says, in one short, kind line, that it might be a good
moment to sit back for a bit. When you sit back a minute after that, it
gives you a short warm word.

What it watches: only what the lamp's eyes already say of the person at the
desk, that your face is nearer the lamp than it was as you sat down this
time (leaning in). It is read against your own sitting, never against a
fixed line, and no picture is kept or sent.

What it says and when:

- Never anything about your body or looks.
- Whether or not someone else is in view, but never while you talk or the
  lamp is in a conversation: it waits until then, for up to ten minutes
  while you are still leaned in, or lets it go.
- A shift of under 20 seconds does not count as sitting back; a minute
  leaned in starts again after you sit back.
- Not again within 30 minutes, at any hour, with no daily most and no
  shared limit with the other wellbeing nudges, but never within a minute
  of another one's line: it waits that minute, then speaks.
- No warm word for coming back after a break: only for sitting back.
- "Not today" or "stop the sit-back reminders" to the lamp snoozes it until
  tomorrow morning or turns it off until you ask for it again. The
  wellbeing nudges share that one function, which stops the last of them.

It keeps nothing about you: only, in memory, when it last went; off is one line with its name.

A core skill, on in the product since its played check with a person on
lamp-9adf (#5208). Its owner can turn it off.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "If you've leaned in a long
while, I'll mention it's a good moment to sit back. Say
'not today' and I'll stop until tomorrow."
