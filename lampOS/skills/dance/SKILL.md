# Dance

Ask the lamp to dance and it answers with one short, playful line, then
dances: about eight seconds of one of its five dances, a slow sway (chill or
waltz) for a calm ask, a bounce (groove or hype) for an upbeat one, and jazz
when you say jazz. Ask for a long one and it dances twice as long. A warm
light comes with it and your own light comes back after.

It never dances on its own, not even to music: it cannot yet tell music from
talk. For 45 seconds after a dance began, it dances again only if what it
heard asks for it ("again", "another", "one more", a dance word); a stray
sound or a question it misheard does not start another. Asked while it
still dances, it says it is still dancing; if you talk over its line, it
stops and listens instead of dancing.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "I dance when you ask me to,
and again whenever you ask for another. Tell me calm, upbeat or jazz if you
have a mood in mind, or I'll pick."
