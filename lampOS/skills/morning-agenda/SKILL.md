# Morning agenda

Once a morning, when you have sat at your desk for two minutes between 7:00
and 11:00, the lamp tells you what is on your Google Calendar today: how
many events, then each one's time and title ("You have 3 events today. At
9:30, Design review. ..."). It needs Google Calendar linked in the app,
under Skills, then Connectors.

When it reads:

- Once a day at most, and only in the morning window.
- Only when you are alone with the lamp, it knows your face as its
  owner's, and nobody has spoken for a minute.
  If that doesn't happen within an hour, it lets the day go and says
  nothing.
- Never in the middle of a conversation, while the lamp is private,
  paused or muted, or at night.
- Never while another skill reads to you, or in the minute after.
- Someone coming into view stops it: "I'll tell you the rest later."

It reads only today's events' times and titles, never a description, a
place's details or who is invited. It keeps nothing of what it read; the
journal keeps only counts.

An installable skill: its owner puts it on from the Skills Store and takes
it off. Not in the product.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "In the morning, once
you've settled at your desk, I'll tell you what's on your calendar today."
