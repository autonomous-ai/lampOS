# Posture

V1's posture reminder. While you sit at the desk with the desk noticing
on, the lamp sends a picture to be labelled every 30 seconds and keeps,
in memory only, whether each one read as a good or a bad way to sit and
which part scored worst (your neck, your back, your shoulders or your
forearms). When the last ten minutes are all in, and at least six in ten
of those readings were bad, the lamp turns to you and says, in one short,
kind line, the part that was most often off and one thing to do about it:

- neck: bring your head back over your shoulders;
- back: sit back and let the chair support it;
- shoulders: let them relax;
- forearms: adjust the keyboard so your elbows rest comfortably;
- no one part: stand up for a short stretch.

It is not Sit back, which is your face coming nearer the lamp as you lean
in; this one is only the posture reading.

What it says and when:

- "A while", never a number of minutes, and never the name of a condition
  or an illness. It never mentions a camera.
- Only when nobody else is in view, never while you talk or the lamp is in
  a conversation, and never right after the lamp spoke.
- Never at night (22:00 to 08:00), and at most one of the wellbeing nudges
  an hour between them. Still sitting badly, it says so again at most
  once an hour.
- "Stop the posture reminders" (or "the slouch ones") to the lamp snoozes
  it until tomorrow morning or turns it off until you ask again. The
  wellbeing nudges share that one function.

Without the desk noticing, or with the pictures not going out, there is no
reading, and it says nothing. It keeps nothing about you: no picture, no
reading after you stand up; off is one line with its name.

A core skill, on in the product. Its owner can turn it off.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "If you've sat badly most
of the last ten minutes, and it's just us, I'll mention what to ease. Say
'not today' and I'll stop until tomorrow."
