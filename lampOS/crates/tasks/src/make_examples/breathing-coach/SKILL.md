# Breathing coach

Said "breathing exercise", the lamp leads three slow breaths: a soft light
rises as you breathe in and fades as you breathe out, with a word for each.

## Uses

- scripts/main.py, no arguments: `lamp.light` and `lamp.say`.

## Result

Nothing is kept or printed: the breaths are led with the light and the voice.

## Answer

The script says each line with lamp.say: "Let's take three slow breaths.", "Breathe in.", "And out.", then "Well done."
