name: radio-stop
description: ALWAYS call this when the words heard include stop, pause, turn off, enough or quiet together with story, radio or drama, in any language ("stop the radio drama", "dừng truyện", "tắt radio"). While a story plays, what you hear is often mixed with the story's own lines, so the text can look long and strange: ignore those lines, and if a request to stop is anywhere in it, call this first. Never answer the story's lines. Then say only a few words.

# Radio stop

Stops the story the radio app is playing; its place is kept.
